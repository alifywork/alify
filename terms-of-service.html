Section 01

Statements of Work & Sprint Governance

All commercial design, branding, and software engineering engagements with ALIFY are governed by a mutually executed Statement of Work (SOW) defining exact deliverables, sprint milestones, and acceptance criteria.

Section 02

100% Client IP & Source Code Ownership

Upon full settlement of milestone invoices, 100% of custom UI designs, brand assets, Figma token systems, and production source code created specifically for your engagement transfer cleanly to your organization.

ALIFY retains no lock-in licensing fees over bespoke client deliverables.

Engineering Quality & Speed Standards
