Data Protection & Privacy

Transparent Data Stewardship & Client Privacy.

How ALIFY Studio collects, safeguards, and processes inquiry information, project specifications, and analytics across our digital platforms.

Effective Oct 2026 GDPR & NDA Aligned

Document Index

Last Updated: October 2026
  • 01. Information We Collect
  • 02. How We Use Your Information
  • 03. Client Confidentiality & NDA Protection

Section 01

Information We Collect

When you submit a project inquiry, estimate request, or consultation booking on ALIFY platforms, we collect only the business contact details you voluntarily provide — such as your name, work email, phone/WhatsApp number, company name, and project scope notes.

We do not sell, rent, or broker personal or corporate data to third-party advertisers under any circumstances.

Section 02

How We Use Your Information

Submitted information is used strictly to evaluate your project requirements, prepare tailored architectural proposals, schedule discovery calls, and deliver contracted design and engineering services.

Aggregated, anonymized performance telemetry helps our engineering team maintain sub-second page speeds and resolve browser issues.

Section 03

Client Confidentiality & NDA Protection

Every project brief, product roadmap, Figma prototype, and repository shared with ALIFY is treated as strictly confidential from first contact — even prior to executing a formal Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (MNDA).

Section 04

Data Security & Access Controls

ALIFY enforces encrypted HTTPS transport, role-based access controls, hardware two-factor authentication across studio tooling, and zero unnecessary retention of sensitive credentials.

Section 05

Your Rights & Contact

You may request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your inquiry records at any time by contacting info@alify.site. Our team responds to all privacy requests within 48 business hours.
